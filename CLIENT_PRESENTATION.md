# Estela LLC Website Prototype Presentation

## Concept

The prototype presents Estela LLC as a modern e-commerce growth partner for brands, manufacturers, distributors, and online retailers.

The visual direction is dark, technical, and premium: black graphite background, restrained cyan and emerald accents, floating navigation, and a marketplace network visual that supports the company's positioning without distracting from the message.

## Core Message

Estela LLC helps companies launch, scale, and optimize sales across Amazon and other global online marketplaces.

The site emphasizes three ideas:

- Technology-minded marketplace growth
- Practical partnership and operational support
- Global sourcing, logistics, and expansion expertise

## Page Structure

- Hero: primary positioning, consultation CTA, and service focus metrics
- About: company profile and global operations expertise
- Services: Amazon launch, listing optimization, PPC, SEO, competitor analysis, FBA, international expansion, and account management
- Platforms: Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, Etsy, Kaufland, Allegro, and other marketplaces
- Why Work With Us: marketplace experience, data-driven growth, global operations, and partner network
- Contact: consultation request form, business location, email, and working hours
- Legal: Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions are available from the footer as modal dialogs

## Implementation Notes

The current version is a static one-page prototype with responsive layout, smooth navigation, working legal dialogs, and prototype form behavior.

Recommended next steps:

- Connect the form to email, CRM, or backend processing
