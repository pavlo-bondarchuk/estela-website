About Estela LLC

Technology-minded e-commerce support for brands moving across markets.

Estela LLC is an e-commerce consulting company specializing in marketplace growth strategies for brands, manufacturers, distributors, and online retailers.

The company supports businesses in launching, managing, and expanding their presence on major online marketplaces including Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and other international platforms.

Beyond marketplace management, Estela brings experience in international sourcing, supply chain coordination, logistics, procurement, and trusted partner networks across Europe, China, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and other global markets.
